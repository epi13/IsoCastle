# Campaign: The Bell Below

## Premise

After years away, the protagonist returns to Greywake to settle the workshop of their missing aunt, Maelin Vey. During the welcome supper, an aurora bends downward and every iron object in town rings once. Maelin's sealed compass points inland toward the drowned monastery of Orrenfast. The disturbance is not a prophecy: Maelin deliberately interrupted an ancient machine and needs help finishing what she began.

## Act I — The Borrowed Note

Greywake and the Bracken March teach survival, factions, and investigation. Beneath Orrenfast lies the first Underbell tier, where the player learns that an order called the Quiet Ledger once used a buried resonator to pull grief from living minds. Its discarded memories became the Choir-in-Stone. The player finds Maelin's trail and chooses whether Greywake's warding iron belongs to the townsfolk, miners, or marsh keepers.

## Act II — Winter Owes a Debt

The trail crosses the Lorn Shelf, where unnatural stillness freezes motion before water. Rival expeditions seek the resonator's three tuning names. Maelin is found alive but partially bound to the Choir. The player can trust her dangerous plan, work with skeptical lore-keeper Senn, or bargain with the Choir's most coherent voice, Little Ash.

## Act III — Rime-Crown

The wind-carved citadel above the Shelf contains the Bell's skyward counterweight. The final descent and ascent interleave remembered Greywake spaces with supernatural battlefields. The player can silence the Bell and return the stolen grief, retune it as a communal archive, or break the mechanism and let its minds become independent. Earlier faction and character choices alter who helps, who survives, and how Greywake changes.

## Endings

The Quiet ending restores private grief at a cost to Maelin. The Archive ending preserves chosen memories through consent and shared stewardship. A difficult Free Choir variation releases the minds but leaves magic unpredictable. Each ending includes a post-game Greywake state, companion outcomes, faction summary, and seed record.
